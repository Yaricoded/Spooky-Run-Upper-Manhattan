Morris-Jumel paranormal investigations

Ongoing program · check the dates

The Morris-Jumel Mansion's site says its "Paranormal Historical Investigations" and some community programs continue while the mansion is closed for roof work. The mansion was expected to reopen "some time in fall 2026," so check the Programs and Events page for dates and whether interior tours are back.

The short version, about 2.5 miles

Ride the 1 train past the long leg

The leg from the Morris-Jumel Mansion to the Dyckman Farmhouse is the longest, about 2.6 miles. Run the first three stops, then ride the 1 train north to a Broadway station near W 204th St (check MTA service) and run the last short leg. That cuts the run to about 2.5 miles of running.
